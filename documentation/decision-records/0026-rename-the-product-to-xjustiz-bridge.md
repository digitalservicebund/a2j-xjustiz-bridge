# Rename the Product to XJustiz-Bridge

## Context and Problem Statement

The naming of the XJustiz-Converter starts to lead to confusion in the external
communication. The official XJustiz-Tools service has some features which are
also labeled as conversion. Furthermore, we started to integrate with the
XJustiz-Tools for XML generation, amplifying the former point. Finally, the
Bundesministerium der Justiz und für Verbraucherschutz made the request to
change the name of the XJustiz-Converter, to improve communication.

Which new name should we use for the product formerly named the XJustiz-Tools?

## Decision Drivers

- No confusion with the XJustiz-Tools and its features
- Not too official sounding name that would imply broader scope
- Describing the capabilities the product actually provides

## Considered Options

- XJustiz-Bridge
- XJustiz-Composer
- XJustiz-Conductor
- XJustiz Conformity Clerk
- XJustiz Mapping Assistent

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "XJustiz-Bridge", because it should cause the least amount of
confusion and conflicts in the external communication. It does not interfere
with any feature or keyword by the XJustiz-Tools. Furthermore, it doesn't sound
too official and generic to the XJustiz standard. Finally, it might be
interpreted with the image of bringing the online services closer to XJustiz
— bridging troubled water. Some might even say it is a bridge to the
XJustiz-Tools.

### Consequences

- Good, because we avoid further confusion in our external communication
- Neutral, because it doesn't describe too well what the product is doing
- Bad, because the original name has already spread and renaming is quite some effort
